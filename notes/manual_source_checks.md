# Sources checked from copies supplied by hand (September 2026)

These sites block automated access, so the text was pasted or saved to PDF and read in full.

| Source | Result |
|---|---|
| SF Planning Code Sec. 148 (amlegal, current codified text) | Identical to Ord. 245-25: applies to new buildings over 85 ft in C-3, Van Ness SUD, Folsom and Main SUD, DTR and Central SoMa SUD; Nine-Hour Hazard Criterion = 26 mph equivalent wind speed for 9+ h/yr. No comfort criterion. |
| SF Planning Code Sec. 139 (amlegal, current, amended by Ord. 56-13, eff. 4/27/2013) | Feature-related hazards cover glazing only (glass walls, wind barriers, skywalks, balconies, rooftop greenhouses, unbroken glazed segments >= 24 sq ft), citywide. New finding: location-related standards (c)(1) apply inside open spaces >= 2 acres dominated by vegetation or open water ("Urban Bird Refuge") and to buildings < 300 ft from one; (c)(1)(B): "Minimal lighting shall be used. Lighting shall be shielded. No uplighting shall be used." Cityside Park and the pad (~90 m from the Bay) meet both conditions, which conflicts with the proposal's two in-grade uplights if the standard is applied to the sculpture. |
| Solaracks, C4 vs C5 (PDF) | Gives ISO 9223 categories (C5 "coastal areas with high salinity"; zinc loss 4.2-8.4 um/yr) and "Within 200 metres of an open coast with onshore winds, chloride deposition rates can reach levels that define a C5 environment." Does not say "C5 usually within 0.5-1 km" (the old doc wording). |
| MDPI Wind 2025, 5(3), 19, INVELOX review (PDF) | "The speed ratio ... is always positive, with [8] suggesting a value between 1 and 2.6"; individual studies up to 2.3 and "up to 3". Matches the doc. |
